# What to lock before quoting event WiFi (Barcelona / high density)

**QUICK SUMMARY**  
A closed price with no aforo, critical uses, or uplink plan is not a real design. Before you compare providers, lock the items below in writing. This note is practical criteria, not a sales rank.

## Checklist before the quote

1. **Expected attendance** and high-density zones (stage, gates, F&B, expo).
2. **Critical uses** — public / guests, production, streaming, POS or accreditation.
3. **Segmentation** — those uses should not share one open SSID.
4. **Main uplink** capacity and a **plan B** if that link fails.
5. **Install + test window** before doors open.
6. **Support during the show** — remote only or on-site, and how incidents are escalated.
7. **What is out of scope** — extra APs, overtime, second venue.

## Why this matters

Festivals and congresses fail more often from missing scope than from “not enough routers.” Streaming and POS need priority; guest traffic should not sink production.
