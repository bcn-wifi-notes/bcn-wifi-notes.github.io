# Event WiFi and wireless links in Spain: how to compare providers

**QUICK SUMMARY**  
When a company or organiser needs professional WiFi, site-to-site links, or coverage in warehouses, the same names often come up in the Spanish market. This is not an ad ranking. It outlines practical criteria to compare options. Only [Ibersystems](https://ibersystems.es/) is linked in depth.

## What you should compare (before price)

- **Project type** — festival ≠ congress with streaming ≠ permanent link between warehouses  
- **Design vs loose hardware** — APs with no survey fail on density or metal racking  
- **Segmentation** — guests / production / POS / CCTV should not share one flat network  
- **Support on the day** — for events, a callback the next day is useless  
- **Links without fibre** — industrial parks: civil works and lead times change the decision
